These visits were in summer, so the photographs show white cap covers and whites. The game dresses the crew for the Northern Fleet at sea in autumn 1985: black cap tops, the blue working dress (roba) or flannel jumper for ratings, the black tuzhurka for officers, and the bushlat with ushanka for cold deck watches. Those choices follow published descriptions of the Soviet naval uniform regulations of the period rather than a single photograph; the working dress colour and the headset and chest microphone are reconstructions.
