HOW TO FIRE
  1. Designate click a track on the radar or in TRACKS (Tab cycles). ◇ ship, △ aircraft or missile.
  2. Choose the weapon GRANIT for ships; FORT (long range) or OSA (short range) for aircraft and missiles.
  3. Acquire track the fire-control radar locks on.
  4. Prepare the launcher readies the rounds; nothing is fired yet.
  5. Arm closes the firing circuit.
  6. Fire releases the salvo. Enter always does the next step.
COMMAND REFERENCE

Your ship. Your orders.

Designate a contact on the radar, select a weapon and salvo size, then ACQUIRE TRACK. Once tracking is established, PREPARE the launcher, ARM the circuit and authorize FIRE. ABORT cancels unfired rounds. Enter always performs the next step. The line under FIRE CONTROL says what to do next and why a step is blocked; each weapon card shows whether it can engage the designated contact. Every weapon has its own AUTO / MANUAL switch on its card (Shift+A for the selected weapon): on AUTO its crew engages by itself, Granit or Bazalt at hostile ships, Fort and Osa at aircraft and missiles, and leaves the target of your own manual sequence alone. The missiles start on MANUAL, the AK-630 close-in guns on AUTO (G). The AUTO button above sets every weapon automatic, or all manual.
